# Hairpin trace operator: development self-test (dev-hairpin sibling)

Provenance: Project HERCULES | [id removed]. Tool: `tools/unr/hairpin_trace.py` (DECL-UNR-1 item 3 as re-read by DECL-UNR-1-A1; native 8.64 um, OME level 0, chunks 128^3). Assembly: `tools/unr/hairpin_selftest.py`. No letters and no readings are claimed; the phantom result is evidence about code paths only, never about PHerc0268.

## What the operator does (one apex, one trace section, a 16-section y-slab)
1. Per section: stripe removal (column z-median), anisotropic structure tensor (derivative sigma 1.5 vox; integration along the preliminary lamina tangent sigma 12 vox, across it sigma 3 vox, through a bank of 12 oriented kernels), validity = tensor energy >= 0.25 x its in-mask median, ridge image g = Gaussian(sigma 3.5) of the stripe-removed section, director-index maps at R = 0.5 and 1.0 mm on a stride-4 grid (topo_axis.index_map); +1/2 plateaus (0.3 < index <= 0.75) merged within R are the apexes, -1/2 plateaus the lobe boundaries.
2. Opening direction of the +1/2: the model psi = phi/2 + c fitted with a free centre on an annulus 0.4-1.0 R0 about the plateau centroid; arms along +d, turn at -d; the fitted centre is the apex.
3. Chord: perpendicular to d at L = 0.8 mm from the apex, half-length S = 1.5 mm; lamina crossings = peaks of g with prominence >= 3 x noise MAD (noise MAD = MAD of the adjacent-section difference of g / sqrt 2, slab-wide), width >= 3, separation >= 8 vox; arm A = s < 0, arm B = s > 0, ordinal 1 = innermost on each arm.
4. For every arm-A crossing (the lamina's last observed point): 2-D ridge following along the tangent (step 1 vox; a turn > 25 deg per step is a junction and the follower coasts; re-anchor to the nearest ridge maximum within +-5 vox along the normal, at most 2 vox per step; lost after 15 steps below 3 noise MAD) until the trace re-crosses the chord on arm B (the first observed point of the paired lamina, matched to the nearest crossing within max(4, min(8, 0.4 x spacing)) vox); the reverse trace from that lamina must return to the same arm-A crossing with mean polyline offset <= 3 vox.
5. Acceptance (status HAIRPIN-TRACED): the region enclosed by the trace and the chord contains exactly one +1/2 (within 12 vox of the region: the innermost loop carries its defect on itself; the strict count is reported too) and no -1/2 (strict); no seam (>= 100 px of dark gap at least 24 vox wide inside the region other than the nest's core void, the wide-dark component nearest the apex); ridge fraction >= 0.9; the trace passes no closer than nb_mm = 1.0 mm to any -1/2 (the min distance is reported so the declaration's max(3 mm, packet thickness) can be applied afterwards); the same ridge re-traced from the same chord crossing is continuous over >= N = 8 consecutive sections of the slab (end within the match tolerance, mean polyline offset <= 3 vox per section).
6. Output per accepted trace: polyline (window and absolute level-0 z,y,x), enclosed-defect counts, ridge continuity (consecutive sections, per-section offsets, ridge fraction), arm pairing (ordinal and position on each arm, above/below), nesting_equal. Every attempt is recorded with its failure reason. HAIRPIN-VERIFIED is never issued here (W14 review is a separate step).

## Reading of the real-data result
At y = 5820 the left-edge column of PHerc0268 at 8.64 um does not present closed 180-degree U-turns whose laminae are continuous ridges from one arm to the other within 0.8 mm of a +1/2: the +1/2 found at R = 0.5 mm are mostly dipole partners of -1/2 (buckles, kinks and lobe boundaries; the R = 1.0 mm count is 3.2x smaller), and the ridge following loses the lamina at junctions within ~90 vox (0.8 mm) in the median. This is a measurement about this y-slab and these parameters, not a statement that hairpins do not exist elsewhere or at other apex radii; the operator's gates reject rather than bridge, as DECL-UNR-1 item 3 requires (default at every fold: TERMINATED).

## Deviations from the declaration (and definitions the declaration leaves open)
1. Scale: the declaration's numbers for the operator were written for level 2 (sigma across the normal <= 0.8 vox, along the tangent 3-4 vox, re-anchoring within min(1.5 vox, 0.3 x spacing), cut after >= 3 lost points); they are scaled x4 to the native 8.64 um here (sigma 3 / 12 vox, re-anchor <= 2 vox per step within a 5-vox search, lost after 15 steps). DECL-UMB-2 names 192^3 level-0 chunks; they are 128^3 (UNR-X0-SURVEY-RESULT item 3).
2. "3 MAD": the MAD of the in-mask ridge image or of a profile is signal-dominated (a regular lamination has peak prominence of only ~2 such MADs, so '3 MAD' rejects every regular lamina, phantom and real); the operator reads it as 3 x the noise MAD of the ridge image (adjacent-section difference). Both MADs are recorded in every JSON.
3. Seam: not defined in-section by the declaration (X1 has SEAM-OPEN/CLOSE events); defined here as a dark gap at least 24 vox (0.2 mm) wide inside the enclosed region other than the nest's core void.
4. Neighbourhood radius to a -1/2: the declaration's max(3 mm, packet thickness) would reject every trace in a 6.6 x 8.8 mm window; the operator uses nb_mm = 1.0 mm by default and reports the minimum distance of every trace, so the declared radius can be applied downstream.
5. "+1/2 enclosed": the defect of the innermost loop lies on that loop itself and its plateau centroid is biased ~20 vox toward the turn by the invalid core void (phantom), so the count uses the region dilated by 12 vox; the strict count is reported beside it.
6. N (consecutive sections) is a parameter (default 8 of the 16-section slab), provisional as the declaration says.
7. The chord replaces the X1 tracker's "last OBSERVED point" (no X1 tracker exists yet): the operator is standalone and finds the arm laminae on a chord at L = 0.8 mm from the apex.
8. The forward/reverse agreement test and the junction coasting rule are additions (not in the declaration); both only reject.
