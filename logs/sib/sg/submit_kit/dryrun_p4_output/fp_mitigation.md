## False-positive mitigation (TP-13, TP-14, TP-15, TP-16)

Null: 44 census tiles were shown rotated a quarter turn (null N0). A census tile counted as positive only with rows and at least the larger of 5 letters and the N0 99th percentile (3 letters).

Confirmation: 2 of 3 fresh readers named at least 5 letters on the candidate; the best pair agreed on 6 same-identity letters within 150 px, against a maximum of 2 over 16 second-phase null tiles. Positive control read with rows in every counted session: yes.

Training separation (TP-15): the official model was trained on labelled Paris 4 segments; the candidate tile's minimum three-dimensional distance to them is 4.8 mm (tiles closer than 1.2 mm were excluded by declaration).

Held-out validation (TP-16): no ground truth exists for the title region; the known-text strip was read in the same census as an attention check (DECL-P4-2-A1).
