No phantom pass is evidence about 0268 and no phantom counts unless its generator is hashed before the pilot is read and reproduces the measured defect statistics. No schedule or lane split is asserted without the resident bytes (CT + m7 rows, tmpfs, ridge tables, renders) written down and `free -m` >= 8 GB before every row, and no ~1 h / ~2 h figure is more than an estimate until the X0 timed row read.
