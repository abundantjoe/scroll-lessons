1. Geometry this pipeline must handle (measured): former roll axis along y; layers stacked along z over ~90 mm; about 120 fold defects per xz section at 3 mm scale, mostly dipoles, in two or three fold-rich bands and two edge columns; coherent fold axes 20-35 mm long along y; large horizontal seams every 1-4 mm; level 3 resolves packets, not single windings (sheet 100-200 um = 1.5-3 voxels, gaps mostly unresolved). Therefore the working scale is level 2 (34.56 um; sheet 3-6 voxels), streamed in y-rows of 128 sections (1.44 GB each, 24 rows) split across the lane containers; level 1 (17.28 um) only as bounded verification crops; level 0 never densely.
2. X0 prerequisites (recorded before any tracking): a level-2 census along the stacking direction on 20 sections spread over y: pitch, sheet thickness and gap histograms, separability fraction (two peaks with a >= 1-voxel dip), debris density, coherent-ridge coverage per 256^2 tile; one timed level-2 y-row read; the m7 surface volume's level-2 pooling method recorded. If the separable fraction of the mask is < 0.3 the tracker is not launched and the census is the result.
3. X1 sheet-identity field (level 2, xz sections tracked along y): per section stripe removal (column z-median), oriented anisotropic structure tensor (sigma across the normal <= 0.8 vox, along the tangent 3-4 vox), rays along the local layering normal with raw-CT lamina/gap peaks primary (prominence >= 3 MAD, width 2-8 vox; > 8 FUSED; < 2 debris) and m7 peaks secondary; linking across sections by gated assignment with gate = 0.5 x locally measured spacing, prediction from the segment's own previous displacement, re-anchoring to the ridge within min(1.5 vox, 0.3 x spacing); events never interpolated: cut after >= 3 lost points or coherence < 0.5, split and contact kept as two ids, break on thickness change > 50 % or displacement > 2 vox, bridging over unobserved gaps <= 2 mm only when both neighbours link; shear-line detector per section with every swept segment cut; independent +y and -y passes, disagreements flagged never averaged; hairpin zones handled by the fold skeleton: a segment entering a fold line's 3 mm neighbourhood is linked to its partner on the other side of the hairpin only if the ridge is continuous through the turn at level 2 (an explicit hairpin trace), else TERMINATED at the fold. Domain: spacing >= 7 vox with a visible gap, coherence >= 0.5, ray-normal angle <= 30 deg. Outputs: per-branch height maps z_k(x, y) with status OBSERVED / BRIDGED / TERMINATED, sparse event table, fragmentation statistics from a 128-section pilot BEFORE any full run.
4. X2 fused-core count: where the census says laminae are fused, sheets are counted by along-stacking density periodicity inside 1 mm slabs at level 0/1 windows, with its own confidence; no winding number is claimed across a FUSED slab.
5. X3 half-layer orientation alternation: gated by an existence test first (in-plane power spectrum of >= 20 clean sheets at level 0 per depth quartile against a phase-randomised null; the two quartiles must differ by ~90 degrees above the null, else the stage is closed with the number recorded); if open, the operator, grammar (R|V one sheet, V|R contact, R|R or V|V fold or cleavage, kollesis exception) and outputs are as in audit_spec.md X3.
6. X4 grow and render: per traced sheet piece, a sparse level-0 copy of the surface volume zeroed outside +-0.5 x local spacing of the X1 height map; vc_grow_seg_from_seed in seed mode with the existing ring parameters; an unmasked far-side grow per patch; arc-length parametrisation along the tracked polyline at assembly; render masks carry BRIDGED / TERMINATED / UNVERIFIED; pieces.py stays the trust unit.
7. X5 frames and winding numbers: per packet, a branch-local ordinal index from the packet's top/bottom envelope; a closure integer per tangent-tracked sheet (+1 expected); cross-packet offsets only by a traced hairpin or a loop count around a terminating fault, else UNKNOWN; absolute winding numbers only for an adjacency component in which every offset is known and every cycle closes. Everything else is reported as (packet id, local index). The orchestrator claims no letters.
8. Bars (W1-W12 of audit_spec.md, unchanged, with the geometry-specific additions): W1 synthetic suite on a flattened concertina phantom (>= 192 sections, packets folded back and forth, sheared cracks, fused sectors, delamination, a translational fault, a debris-filled crack along y, 1.5-voxel sheets, x-stripes, integer-spacing shear): correct bridge >= 95 % of cracks <= 2 mm, false termination on hairpins <= 5 %, 0 bridges across fused sectors, hairpin partner linking correct on >= 95 % of traced turns, injected one-sheet jumps flagged on 100 %; W2 index continuity along the mesh >= 98 % of vertices in the modal class per piece, 0 index steps inside a piece; W3 neighbour clearance >= 0.5 x local spacing for >= 98 % of vertices; W4 coverage per 5 mm band reported with every alignment number; W5 raw-only vs m7-only agreement reported; W6 masked vs unmasked grow >= 95 % within 0.25 x spacing; W7 fibre-bundle continuity across BRIDGED cracks >= 80 % above the one-spacing null; W8 forward/backward and +y/-y agreement median <= 0.5 vox, p95 <= 1.5 vox; W9 drift <= 2 vox at level 2 for 95 % of chained blocks; W10 normal gate against the fold-skeleton frame (withheld where the frame has no line within 10 mm); W11 X3 metrics; W12 an external reference-only run on a scroll with organizer segments (PHerc0343P or PHerc0009B) reproducing >= 90 % of an organizer sheet's vertices within 0.25 x spacing. Any failed bar stops the stage; nothing is lowered; a failed pilot is the deliverable of that stage.
9. Order and compute: X0 census (one lane, ~1 h); X1 pilot on 128 sections centred on the longest fold-free band (one lane, ~2 h); bars W1 (phantom, local) and W8/W9/W12 on the pilot before any full pass; the full X1 pass on 7 lanes (3-4 y-rows each) only after the pilot passes; X4 renders per validated piece; the ink model (DECL-FL-7) is applied to rendered pieces only after X4's bars, with the FL-7 bars unchanged.
