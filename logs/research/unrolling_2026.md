## 0. Summary: the five most portable techniques

1. Overlapping-patch 2D/3D inconsistency test (W. Stevens, reports 10 and 12, pipeline9): two patches that share 2D coordinates but whose 3D points diverge along the normal beyond a threshold mark a sheet switch; it needs no roll model, so it transfers to the flattened stack as-is. Test: fraction of X4 pieces with a normal-projected divergence > a declared threshold at any shared 2D point.
2. vc_tifxyz_selfcross both-diagonal transverse-contact census (villa docs) plus the vc_tifxyz_topology islands/holes/tears/folds census (PR #1884, draft): report-only, deterministic, no distance threshold, built for crushed scrolls where wrap proximity is normal. Test: zero transverse contacts under both triangulations and zero tears before any piece is rendered.
3. vc_calc_surface_metrics with inter-winding ground-truth point collections (villa docs): surface_missing_fraction, winding_error_fraction, in_surface_metric against points placed along the normal across several windings. Test: annotate inter-winding points along z in the level-0 windows and score every assembled piece.
4. Ray-along-normal intersection counting with integer consistency (FASP winding assignment, vc_tifxyz_winding; ScrollFiesta's pairwise integer winding relations with loop closure): the ray-hit count between two verified laminae is a direct stack count. Test: integer agreement between the ray-hit count and the X2 thickness/pitch count, and zero loop-closure conflicts.
5. Fibre continuity as same-sheet evidence (villa Automated Fiber Volumes generator PR #1934; spiral-fitting 'strips and fibers following a single sheet'; atlas links with desired_winding_delta): automated fibres with 30-voxel joins and 45-degree kink cuts, traced through a rendered piece or through a level-0 hairpin window. Test: fraction of fibres that continue across a piece seam or a hairpin apex without a cut.

Runner-up: neural copy tracer hop (copy_displacement_latest with the PR #1284 displacement scale) as an adjacent-sheet proposal, scored by a wrong-hop rate against the X1 lamina peaks.

## 1. Sheet identity tracking (which voxels or patches belong to the same sheet)

### 1.1 Overlapping-patch 2D/3D inconsistency and simulated annealing (William Stevens)
- What it does: grows small octagonal patches from a surface-prediction zarr (diameter 180-500 voxels, quadmesh spacing 4 voxels; 1.7-4.7 mm at 9.362 um), aligns each overlapping pair by a rigid 2D transform, and flags inconsistencies "by projecting the 3D vector between two points having the same 2D coordinates onto the surface normals of each patch, and checking whether the length of either vector is greater than some threshhold" (report 12). Three detectors: chains of 2-5 patches; "bridges" (a patch B in a chain A,B,C where dist(A,C) exceeds diameter(B) plus the radii of A and C); simulated annealing on the flattened rendering, cost = flat area minus inconsistency-weighted red points. Report 12 states the method "doesn't make any assumption about it being a rolled up sheet".
- Input scale: 9.362 um surface prediction (PHerc0139, Scroll 4). Report 10 used a divergence threshold of 50 voxels on Scroll 4 and found a missed case where patches "don't diverge by more than 41 units".
- Stated validation: Scroll 4, 56,968 patches, 9,171 flagged (16%); PHerc0139 first 2,858 patches, 456 bad (16%), 19 bridges, "a 3-winding unwrapping that is mostly free from sheet switching problems"; report 10: 94,142 patches, 1,153 problem patches at threshold 50, false positives and manually found false negatives acknowledged. No ground-truth rate is given.
- URLs: https://github.com/WillStevens/scrollreading/blob/main/report12.pdf (18 July 2026), https://github.com/WillStevens/scrollreading/blob/main/report10.pdf (27 Dec 2025), https://github.com/WillStevens/scrollreading/tree/main/pipeline9 ; prizes: https://scrollprize.org/winners (Jul 2026 $2,500; Aug 2026 $20,000).
- Portable test (one line): for every pair of overlapping X4 pieces, normal-projected 3D divergence at shared 2D points; declare the threshold before data in local-pitch units (at 8.64 um one pitch is 44 voxels), report the flagged fraction and inspect each flag in a level-1 crop.

### 1.2 vc_tifxyz_selfcross (official, villa) and windcheck (community)
- What it does: "reports where a tifxyz surface passes through itself"; both-diagonal triangulation, non-adjacent transverse contacts only (coplanar and grazing contacts reported separately, never counted); no distance threshold because "wrap proximity is normal in a crushed scroll"; quads with any edge > --maxedge (default 60 voxels) dropped; --exclude default 1; exit 3 with --fail-on-crossing; deterministic across thread counts.
- Input scale: any tifxyz; the doc's corpus note is a 185-trace set where treating z <= 0 as valid changed counts for 1.49% of rows.
- Stated validation (windcheck, using the official tool as the referee): "274/274 clean under both canonical triangulations" across "456,475,808 triangles"; area-weighted retention 0.994566, median area cost 0.083%; 84,316 PHercParis4 patches audited, five self-intersect. Limitation stated by windcheck: "Nothing here establishes which physical sheet a surface belongs to."
- URLs: https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/volume-cartographer/docs/tifxyz_selfcross.md ; https://github.com/joe-carr-data/windcheck (Jul 2026 $1,000, https://scrollprize.org/winners).
- Portable test: run vc_tifxyz_selfcross on every assembled piece and on every pair of neighbouring pieces merged (vc_merge_tifxyz) before render; gate = 0 transverse contacts under both triangulations at --exclude 1 and a --maxedge set to one local pitch (44 voxels), recorded per piece. This is the external self-intersection test DECL-UNR-1 item 6 already requires.

### 1.3 vc_tifxyz_topology (draft PR #1884, not merged)
- What it does: report-only grid-topology census: islands (valid quads outside the largest edge-connected component), holes (enclosed runs of invalid cells), tears (grid edges long relative to the surface's own median step), folds ("quads whose two triangles face more than 120 degrees apart"); --fail-on exits 3.
- Stated validation (author): PHerc0009B census of 7 components and 1,423 island quads matched vc_flatten on main dropping 2,846 triangles; 829 of 907 published tifxyz directories across 11 scrolls in 145.6 s single-threaded; 21 unit/CLI tests. Status: Draft, opened 24 Sep 2026, not reviewed.
- URL: https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/pull/1884
- Portable test: tears = 0 and islands = 0 on every piece; the fold count is a hairpin detector for any mesh that is allowed to turn through the apex (a true hairpin quad straddling the apex faces > 120 degrees), so folds are expected only at HAIRPIN-VERIFIED locations and nowhere else.

### 1.4 tifxyz-doctor (community)
- What it does: read-only QA of tifxyz: metadata/contract checks; distortion metrics per cell (bilinear-centre Jacobian condition, symmetric stretch, area ratio, shear, symmetric Dirichlet energy, direction-specific edge-length deviations); topology cues (degenerate or folded triangles, adjacent-cell normal jumps, coherent bands with a large normal component, sampled non-local proximity).
- Stated validation: on a 128-patch hold-out with synthetic defects the v0.2 cue "localized 124/128 abrupt 8-voxel normal-offset proxies and 128/128 abrupt 16-voxel proxies"; "gradual four- and twelve-cell transitions were mostly missed"; the README states there is "no authoritative labeled corpus" of correct/incorrect geometry.
- URL: https://github.com/aviad12g/tifxyz-doctor (Jul 2026 $1,000).
- Portable test: per-cell symmetric Dirichlet energy and adjacent-cell normal-jump maps on each piece (mesh distortion metric); at 8.64 um an 8-16 voxel normal offset is 0.2-0.4 pitch, so the proxy sensitivity is at the sub-pitch step a sheet switch produces.

### 1.5 vc_calc_surface_metrics and vc_tifxyz_winding (official, villa)
- What it does: scores a tifxyz against a ground-truth point collection annotated in VC3D with relative winding numbers: surface_missing_fraction ("fraction of inter-winding GT points that were completely missing in surface"), winding_error_fraction ("fraction of inter winding GT points that were incorrect (missing points are also incorrect)"), in_surface_metric ("fraction of intra-winding GT points that were correct"). Inter-winding points are placed "roughly along the surface normal" across several windings; winding.tif comes from vc_tifxyz_winding.
- vc_tifxyz_winding (source read): 500 random seed cells; for each, a line along the grid normal; 1,000 candidate cells refined by pattern search, hit if point-to-line distance <= 0.5, duplicates within 4 px dropped; per-winding x-spacing = median over 400-px column bands; propagation from one seed accepts a consecutive-hit pair only if |dx - ref| <= ref/3, assigning winding +-1; a FIXME notes propagation runs in one direction only; no umbilicus is read.
- Stated validation: none in the doc (no thresholds).
- URL: https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/volume-cartographer/docs/surface_metrics.md ; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ScrollPrize/villa/main/volume-cartographer/apps/src/vc_tifxyz_winding.cpp
- Portable test: in each level-0 window place inter-winding GT points along z through the stack (the stacking direction replaces the radial direction) and intra-winding points along a traced lamina; score every piece; declared gate on winding_error_fraction before data. This is a direct measure of index continuity (W2) that does not use X1's own map.

### 1.6 VC3D GrowPatch/tracer losses (Oct 2025) and normal grids
- What it does: patch growth gained a normal-alignment loss built on "a fast approximate skeletonization trace of surface prediction slices", a snapping loss "Based on the same skeletonization as the normals", a direction-field term that "penalizes deviation from a dense direction field" (from fibre predictions or structure-tensor orientations), and a symmetric Dirichlet barrier against too-small quads; human corrections "reduce sheet switches and help the mesh adhere more tightly". Point corrections are run with --resume and --correct; normal grids must exist (vc_compute_normal_grids) or be streamed via normal-grids-remote.json.
- Stated validation: qualitative only ("reduce sheet switches"); the Feb 2025 post reports sheet jumps in an 800 cm2 Scroll 5 crop going from "around 11" to 0 in two weeks of iteration, by eye.
- URLs: https://scrollprize.substack.com/p/multiple-scrolls-now-show-greek-letters (9 Oct 2025); https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/volume-cartographer/docs/grow_patch_point_corrections.md ; https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/volume-cartographer/docs/extending_and_modifying_segmentations.md ; https://scrollprize.substack.com/p/new-prizes-and-an-update-on-progress (27 Feb 2025).
- Portable test: feed the tracer's direction-field term the 8.64 um structure-tensor orientation (tools/umb/st_axis.py) instead of fibre predictions, and keep the existing DECL-FL-4-A5 gate (median |cos(normal, layering normal)| >= 0.8, frac > 0.8 >= 0.7) as the acceptance test.

### 1.7 Neural copy tracer (copy_displacement_latest) and displacement rescaling (PR #1284)
- What it does: "From an input mesh used as conditioning, the model predicts a 3 vector displacement field to the recto surface of the winding" in front of and behind the conditioning mesh (model card); "trained at 4.8um resolution, but performs ok at 2.4um and 9.6um". PR #1284 adds --displacement-scale auto = training voxel size / inference voxel size.
- Stated validation: a "local geometric benchmark rather than ground truth" on one compressed 7.91 um Scroll 3 window: wrong-hop rate 63.17% -> 5.90% (front, n = 4,697) and 56.28% -> 7.73% (back, n = 8,697) after scaling by 4.8/7.91; the judging rule for a wrong hop is in a linked log that was not read (UNVERIFIED). The June 2026 progress post says Neural Tracing "soon drifts off the intended surface" and inference is slow.
- URLs: https://huggingface.co/scrollprize/copy_displacement_latest ; https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/pull/1284 (merged 17 Aug 2026); https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/issues/1149 ; https://scrollprize.substack.com/p/the-hard-parts-are-getting-clearer (5 Jun 2026).
- Portable test: from a verified lamina mesh in a level-0 window, hop to the next lamina along +z and -z; a hop is right when it lands within 0.5 local pitch of the X1 lamina peak and wrong otherwise; report the wrong-hop rate per window at displacement scale 4.8/8.64 = 0.556 and at 1.0.

### 1.8 nnInteractive sheet refinement (Aug 2026 prize)
- What it does: from one mask covering all sheets, connected components (cc3d, face connectivity) give one label per sheet; sheets still joined by a bridge are separated by erosion and reassigned to the nearest eroded core; each sheet is refined with nnInteractive prompts (its mask, the flanking gap centrelines as negative scribbles, its own centreline as positive). Output: one label per sheet plus a JSON with per-sheet voxel counts and Dice against the input.
- Stated validation: none reported (Dice is a report field, not a result).
- URL: https://github.com/hammb/vesuvius-surface-detection-nninteractive-refinement
- Portable test: on PAIR/FUSED runs of X1 in a level-0 window, count labels after refinement and compare with the X2 count; report the integer agreement rate and the per-sheet Dice against the raw-CT ridge mask.

### 1.9 Patch-alignment sheet identity in ScrollFiesta (see 3.2 for the counting side)
- What it does (submission PDF): welding "joins fronts across cube seams only where both cubes agree on the wrap"; ball pivoting is "guarded by anti-parallel and accumulated-winding tests"; identity is carried as "sheet tracks whose pairwise integer winding relations compose through a maximum-support forest with integer subtree-shift loop closure, so geometry is never fused to make an order graph connected". The PDF states the decisive error, "a surface that has stepped onto the neighbouring turn", is "invisible to every geometric quality measure".
- URL: https://github.com/Hob3rMallow/scrollfiesta_public (submission.pdf; docs/PIPELINE.md; docs/SHEET_ASSEMBLY.md). Prizes: May 2026 $10,000, Jul 2026 $20,000, Aug 2026 $2,500 (https://scrollprize.org/winners).
- Portable test: build the piece-to-piece relation graph with integer z-stack offsets (0 = same lamina, +-1 = adjacent) and report the residual conflict weight over all cycles; ScrollFiesta reports 5.0% residual conflict weight on 15,457 tracks over 112 winds (PHerc0139) and continuation/order satisfaction 0.99971/0.99816 on a 4x5x5 block.

## 2. Hairpin continuity (sheet continuity through the nested U-turns at the x edges)

No 2025-2026 release documents a hairpin or fold operator. What exists:

### 2.1 SCROLL_ANATOMY.md (official glossary, villa)
- States: "A fold bends papyrus back onto itself and can involve one or several windings."; folding does not change winding membership; "A sharp bend is not necessarily a fold."; "Near the core of a roll, papyrus may fold back on itself one or more times"; "Compression can bring adjacent windings into contact, reduce or eliminate the space between them". No numbers, no method, no use of the word hairpin.
- URL: https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/SCROLL_ANATOMY.md
- Portable test: none (glossary). It confirms the DECL-UNR-1-A1 reading that continuity through a hairpin keeps sheet identity and recto/verso alternates.

### 2.2 Diffeomorphic spiral fitting (Henderson, WACV 2026; villa/spiral-fitting; PHerc 343 First Letters)
- What it does: fits an extruded Archimedean spiral deformed by "a per-slice affine transform, an integrated flow field, and a gap-scaling field" to U-Net surface and fibre predictions; losses on normals, radii, winding spacing, fibre orientation, verified patches ("a verified patch should coincide with a single winding across its whole extent"), relative and absolute winding annotations; a later distance loss snaps paths onto windings; the umbilicus is required.
- Input scale: PHerc. Paris 4 at 7.91 um (13600 x 4200 x 3600 voxel subvolume), PHerc. 172 qualitative; the PHerc 343 win ran "the villa spiral chain (commit 0fb38c45)" on the 8.64 um scan, z 9500-11000, with the winner's own axis file, then "snap_meshes.py (defaults, level 0)" which "moves every node along its normal onto the nearest predicted sheet" (m7 prediction, level 1 distance field).
- Stated validation: metrics WJF (winding jump fraction: "how often the ground-truth surfaces cross between different windings of our spiral"), MRWD (mean radial winding distance), ChD, AD (angular defect, "should be zero for developable surfaces"), Str; Paris 4 Table 1: ChD 5.29 vs 5.59, AD 0.0568 vs 0.1567, Str 1.239 vs 1.027 against ThaumatoAnakalyptor; MRWD 25.67 without the relative-winding loss vs 7.64 with. Stated limitations: "the surface sometimes wanders between two true windings, instead of committing to one."; "We also currently assume the scroll is a single rolled sheet."; the June 2026 post: the fit "does not conform well where annotations... are missing". The PHerc 343 repository reports reproducibility checks only (bit-identical meshes, r 0.99999 between GPUs), no surface-correctness metric.
- Whether the per-slice affine plus flow can represent a flattened roll with lobed hairpin columns is NOT demonstrated on any page read (UNVERIFIED; no crushed-scroll result is reported). For PHerc0268 the only umbilicus on record is an estimate: PR #1745 (closed, not merged) says "the umbilici are estimates, not tracings" and "PHerc0800 and PHerc0268 have umbilicus files but no validated count yet".
- URLs: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04927 ; https://github.com/pmh47/spiral-fitting ; https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/tree/main/spiral-fitting ; https://scrollprize.org/tutorial_spiral ; https://scrollprize.substack.com/p/50k-first-letters-prize-awarded-for (8 Oct 2026); https://github.com/Nieuwlaar/pherc343-first-letters ; https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/pull/1745
- Portable tests: (a) WJF computed with our level-0 lamina traces as the ground truth for any fitted global sheet; (b) the snap residual: median and p90 node-to-ridge distance (voxels) before and after snapping each piece onto the raw-CT Hessian centrelines of DECL-UMB-2, at 8.64 um.

### 2.3 Fibre tracing as continuity evidence (villa line annotation, Automated Fiber Volumes, atlas links, fibre intersections)
- What it does: VC3D line annotation fills spans between control points by native 3D fibre tracing, Lasagna normal-based optimisation or cubic spline; merge accepted when the meeting error is "at most max(10 base voxels, 10% of the combined partial traced length)"; span actions "Split, different windings" and "Split and link, same winding". Automated Fiber Volumes (.afv, PR #1930, merged 5 Oct 2026) hold whole collections (PHerc. 0175A: 61,486 fibres). The generator (PR #1934, open): nnU-Net fibre model Qualzz20/afv_fiber_9um on 512^3 blocks, skeletonise and fit polylines, join aligned ends up to 30 voxels apart, cut joins turning more than 45 degrees, drop fibres shorter than 100 voxels; example 1,055 fibres (1.87 m) in 1 min 21 s on a 9.362 um PHerc. 0813 zone. Fibre intersections are refined by one Ceres problem per candidate, and the operator decides "same winding (h inside v)", "different winding" or "hard to say"; only the first appends an atlas link (desired_winding_delta = 0). The June 2026 post: an annotator traces a fibre at "2 cm / minute" where the latent holds.
- Stated validation: the .afv generator was checked for "identical polylines and identical stitched fibers" against the code it was ported from; no accuracy against ground truth on any page.
- URLs: https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/volume-cartographer/docs/line_annotation_fibers.md ; https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/pull/1930 ; https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/pull/1934 ; https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/volume-cartographer/docs/fiber_intersections.md ; https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/volume-cartographer/docs/atlas_implementation.md ; https://scrollprize.substack.com/p/the-hard-parts-are-getting-clearer
- Portable test (fibre continuity): trace fibres at 8.64 um inside a level-0 edge window and inside each rendered piece; a HAIRPIN-VERIFIED link requires at least one fibre polyline that continues around the apex without a > 45-degree cut and with the ends joined within 30 voxels; report the fraction of hairpin candidates so supported and the fraction of piece seams crossed by uncut fibres. The prize page's own Stage-1 guidance is the same check in words: "check if you can visually follow horizontal papyrus fibers across the page".

## 3. Fused-core counting (how many sheets in a PAIR/FUSED run; stack and winding counts)

### 3.1 Ray-along-normal intersection counting (FASP, Jan 2025; vc_tifxyz_winding)
- What it does: FASP's winding assignment "places random points, measures intersections along normals, and diffuses a consistent winding number from a single seed"; vc_tifxyz_winding implements the per-surface version (details in 1.5): hits along the normal line, median per-winding spacing, pair acceptance within ref/3.
- Input scale: FASP ran on half-resolution Scroll 1 volumes (7.91 um metadata); about 28 h compute plus 4 h human input.
- Stated validation: FASP claims "High quality areas (compare state.tif) surpass the GP quality"; no numeric accuracy; three errata (two fixed 2025-01-17).
- URLs: https://github.com/hendrikschilling/FASP ; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ScrollPrize/villa/main/volume-cartographer/apps/src/vc_tifxyz_winding.cpp
- Portable test: between two gap-resolved laminae in a level-0 window, count raw-CT ridge crossings along z (prominence >= 3 MAD, the X1 rule) and compare with run thickness / local pitch (the X2 rule); a count is claimed only when both agree to the integer and the pitch p10-p90 error is < 1 sheet.

### 3.2 ScrollFiesta's probabilistic winding (generalized winding number plus integer MRF) and its negative result on fused runs
- What it does: "A two-sided generalized winding number field supplies noisy absolute per-component observations, local continuation and radial-order measurements supply pairwise factors, and an integer-label Markov random field reconciles both by alpha-beta swap graph cuts"; radial order "is measured from separation across a local pitch rather than from connectivity". Scroll geometry is a parameter (PHerc0139 calibration: --umb-y 3405 --umb-x 2878 --wrap-pitch 9.5).
- Stated validation: full-field probe of 313,615 vertices shows "the clean-sheet unit jump at 310,686 vertices (99.1%)"; 4x5x5 PHerc0139 block: 882,219 vertices, 783 components contracted to 25, energy 221.369 -> 200.486, continuation/order satisfaction 0.99971/0.99816, 10.8 s; 213-cube: 15,457 tracks over 112 winds at 5.0% residual conflict weight. Negative result stated by the authors: "a slab splitter that separates 0 of 246 fused runs"; an unguarded penalty run was rejected because "its winding span expands from 14.13 to 32.41 turns - the penalties bought local agreement by inventing turns". Public configuration: "47.4% source-area coverage, with 171 overlapping triangle pairs", reported "as unqualified".
- URL: https://github.com/Hob3rMallow/scrollfiesta_public (submission.pdf, docs/PIPELINE.md)
- Portable test: the integer MRF's inputs map onto our frame (local pitch along z instead of radius); the measurable port is the loop-closure conflict weight of pairwise integer offsets between pieces (declared gate before data). The 0-of-246 result is direct published evidence that a geometric splitter does not resolve fused runs, which supports DECL-UNR-1 item 4 (no integer claimed when the error >= 1 sheet).

### 3.3 Spiral-fit density terms
- What it does: the fit uses "gradient magnitude (local winding density)" from Lasagna volumes, a "Native minimum-spacing loss", and a "winding_model" mode with "long relative-winding pairs and adjacent-passage density pairs" (24,000 samples per step). The ink-coverage model "was only trained on PHerc. Paris 4".
- Stated validation: none for the density terms as a count.
- URLs: https://scrollprize.org/tutorial_spiral ; https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/tree/main/spiral-fitting
- Portable test: compare the Lasagna gradient-magnitude density in a level-0 window against the X0 census pitch histogram; agreement of the modal spacing within 10% is the only measurable claim available.

## 5. The complete unwrapping of PHerc. 1667 (arXiv 2606.29085, June 2026) and the 2027 Grand Prize bar

- Method as read: nnU-Net-style 3D U-Net surface prediction (Medial Surface Recall, CE, soft Dice) "used only as an intermediate cue for tracing"; tifxyz quad meshes grown from seeds; "A mesh-offset tool traces rays from an existing mesh to new predictions where one winding already has a high-quality mesh"; manual control points, push/pull, local re-optimisation; "Regions judged to be a single sheet receive an approval mask"; small holes by geometric inpainting that "did not create tomographic intensity values, ink signal, or image evidence"; SLIM flattening; 65-layer render at 1-voxel spacing (2.4 um voxels); "~25 hours per wrap of manual annotation". Geometry acceptance: "the sampled surface was geometrically tight to the papyrus layer in the CT volume"; readings accepted if letterforms "remained consistent across repeated renderings or inference passes". No self-intersection, coverage or fibre-continuity metric appears in the text read. Code: https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/commit/e583fb67468f483fadd73d52e06f0ab0fe5ba813

## 6. What was not found (so it is not claimed)
- No published method for a flattened roll with lobed hairpin columns; no hairpin or fold tracer; no fused-lamina counter with a stated accuracy. The only quantitative fused-run result is ScrollFiesta's 0 of 246.
- No 2025-2026 ThaumatoAnakalyptor paper surfaced; it appears only as the baseline in arXiv 2512.04927.
- The Kaggle metric formula, Will Stevens' report 11, the ScrollFiesta 9 um model "distilled from 2.4 um predictions" (winners page line only), the 3D sheet-growing model by M. Nestorovic (winners page line, no link), and Khartes segmata (README has no algorithm) remain thin or unverified and are not ported.
- The June 2026 winners-page entry that names the project's owner is deliberately omitted from this file.

## 7. Minimal porting plan (declarative; no data read yet)
1. Gate every X4 piece with vc_tifxyz_selfcross (both diagonals, --maxedge = one pitch) and the topology census; record counts in the piece's validation.json.
2. Annotate inter-winding GT points along z and intra-winding points along laminae in two level-0 windows chosen by the DECL-UNR-1 item 9 rule; run vc_tifxyz_winding + vc_calc_surface_metrics on each piece; declare the winding_error_fraction gate before the first score.
3. Implement the Stevens 2D/3D divergence test on overlapping pieces with a threshold declared in pitch units; flag, inspect in level-1 crops, never auto-bridge.
4. Ray-along-z crossing counts versus thickness/pitch counts for every PAIR/FUSED run; integer claimed only on agreement.
5. Automated fibre tracing in the edge windows; a hairpin becomes HAIRPIN-VERIFIED only with an uncut fibre around the apex and the 2-D trace already required by DECL-UNR-1.
