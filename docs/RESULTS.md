## How to read this document

- **Labels.** **VERIFIED**: reproduced by an independent code path, by a re-run, or re-checked against a committed
  file. **MEASURED**: one recorded run, not independently repeated. **INFERENCE**: an interpretation of measured
  numbers. Every factual statement carries a label.
- **Citations.** Each `file:line` link points to the line at a fixed commit. The `@hash` after the link is the commit that
  introduced that text. Lines 1–3202 of `RULES.md` are byte-identical at `71720ce` and now, so they link there. Text
  added on 2026-10-15 links to the commit that added it.
- **Quotations, units and nulls.** Text in quotation marks is copied from the cited line, and plain statements of
  what an entry declares are paraphrased from it. The citation is their evidence: VERIFIED by reading the line. The "Question", "Unit · effective n" and "Null" rows give this document's
  reading of each test's design. They are INFERENCE unless they carry their own label and citation.
- **Registration.** PRE-REGISTERED means the bar sits in `RULES.md` above the result and was committed before it. Where
  the declaration and the result first appear in the same commit, git cannot show the order. Each such case is marked
  "pre-registration not confirmable in git". Where git shows the declaration first but only by minutes, the gap is
  given; under five minutes, git alone cannot show that the run started after the declaration.
- **Units of analysis.** A *pixel*, a *0.5 cm² unit* (the frozen rule's square, 755 px at 9.362 µm [`RULES.md:47`](https://github.com/abundantjoe/hercules/blob/71720ce/RULES.md?plain=1#L47) `@8a52745`), a *surface* (one
  traced or published mesh, read once), or a *read*. Units on one surface share geometry, render and scan region. They
  are not independent, and flagged units cluster: the control's 4-unit cluster is a 1-in-39 event under independent
  flagging — MEASURED [`RULES.md:2504`](https://github.com/abundantjoe/hercules/blob/71720ce/RULES.md?plain=1#L2504) `@cf64ed9`. **The known-text control is one surface.** At the surface level its
  effective n is 1.
