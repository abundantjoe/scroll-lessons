# worlds-first: ink-model images of never-read scroll areas

3892 never-before-published images that Dominion made of scroll areas with no published reading: PHerc0268, the
volumes of the First Letters push, and PHerc. Paris 4 (Scroll 1) away from its published reading. Folders: `<scroll>/<branch>/<original path>`;
the branch of the same name holds the experiment that made each image.

**What these are.** Ink-model outputs (probability maps, overlays and the tiles shown to blind readers), not readings.
Our own lessons (see [MASTER_LESSONS.md](../MASTER_LESSONS.md)) show that much apparent ink on thin strips is a rendering artifact.
**No letters are claimed.** A few patches under prize review are withheld.
