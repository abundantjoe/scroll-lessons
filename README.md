## Question

What do scholarship and the organizers' publications say about where an end title sits on a Herculaneum roll, how large it is and what ink it uses? The aim was to direct the Paris 4 title search (DECL-P4-10 item 5). A second order asked which open prizes were the fastest money.

## Method

This was literature-only research by three in-session research subagents (no separate branches): Herculaneum bibliology, ink and pigment archaeometry, and Paris 4 itself. Findings were graded FIRM/LIKELY/UNCERTAIN, and key citations were spot-checked at source before merging into DOSSIER.md. Strip geometry was derived only from committed census counts and mesh vertex coordinates, with no images. The lane also built a prize catalogue from the official organizer pages and drafted three contribution texts for monthly Progress Prizes.

## What ran on this branch

Between 2026-09-26 18:00Z and 2026-09-27 ~01:20Z it produced DOSSIER v1 to v4, PRIZES v2 and the pp_forms drafts. Expected-text material was kept quarantined in a single section. On 2026-09-30 a First Letters pass on PHerc0846A was assigned here. It was not launched: the auto-mode classifier denied the detached pipeline, and the lane did not retry.

## Outcome

DOSSIER v4 (sections 0-7) is complete. It gives the codicological basis for where a title would sit, with weighted prior search windows; a published census of 70 Herculaneum titles, for example, places 91 % of them beside the last column. It also retracted an earlier roll-length figure. PRIZES v2 ranked the monthly Progress Prizes as the fastest route and noted that publishing code needs the owner's approval. Three contribution texts were drafted; submission was left to the owner by hand, and nothing was posted. No experimental verdict.
