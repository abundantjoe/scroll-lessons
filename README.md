# P4-OFF maps, FL-2 PHerc0826, FL-4

**Dominion experiment branch** `claude/gifted-bell-gavnl5-sib-p4v-08` · PARIS-4 investigations · 2026-09-30..2026-10-02 · status **STOPPED** · verdict **INCONCLUSIVE**

Part of [Dominion](https://github.com/abundantjoe/dominion): see the [experiment map](https://github.com/abundantjoe/dominion/blob/main/docs/EXPERIMENTS.md) and the [branch index](https://github.com/abundantjoe/dominion/blob/main/BRANCH_INDEX.md). Scrolls: PHercParis4, PHerc0826, PHerc0268.

## Question

Worker lane with three successive jobs: verso-mode and forward S1 maps of PHerc Paris 4 tiles for P4-OFF (DECL-P4-26-A2); First Letters material on PHerc0826 as its primary worker (DECL-FL-2); then FL-4 Grand Prize-model predictions as a PHerc0268 lane.

## Method

Ran the project's own S1 ink model (sha bfa9f7a6) unchanged through tools/p4/infer_verso.py with reversed layer order (verso face) and in forward order, on an assigned list of PHerc Paris 4 segment tiles, after a byte-identical forward repro of a reference tile as the gate. For FL-2 (official organizer ink_9um step-075000 only, DECL-FL-2-E1) it auto-grew segments from seeds with the organizers' VC3D grower (gate: 0 self-intersections), rendered them, inferred in both directions and cut 2 x 2 cm tiles for the blind-reader census. For FL-4 (DECL-FL-4-A1/-A2) it grew small segments from the declared seed list and ran the organizer-hosted 2023 Grand Prize TimeSformer, variant B (rescale 8.64/7.91), forward then reverse, giving cover cells and a whole-segment window.

FL-2: 4 of its tiles were read in FL-2 wave 1 and 26 of its windows in FL-3 wave 1, with 0 positives fleet-wide under a gate that was NOT SENSITIVE, so the nulls are uninformative; DECL-FL-5 later found all FL-2/FL-3 unread-scroll tiles came from a grower that cuts across windings. Its FL-4 items were read in FL4-W41, W45 and W47 (0 positives) and are VOID (DECL-FL-4-A5). The keepalive was killed at the 7200 s harness limit at 07:59Z on 2026-10-02 and not re-armed, losing the in-flight pass (DECL-FL-4-A4-A2-E4).
